Outside work, I research price-time geometry and market cycles. I wrote Reflexive Symmetrical Architecture (RSA), a paper registered on Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.16749290), and I build tools to test where multi-timeframe orderflow liquidity and cyclic geometry meet.

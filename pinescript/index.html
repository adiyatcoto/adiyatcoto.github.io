Coto's RRA Ultra Granular 12.5 Steps — 2026 Edition
tradingview.com/script/9VlhfNHG · Pine Script v5
Draws range-based support/resistance across 32 ultra-granular steps (12.5% increments up to 400%) calculated from the high/low range of a chosen pivot cycle — from daily up to 250-year blocks, with optional manual start-year alignment (e.g. halving cycles). Includes a Dynamic Equilibrium (EQ) midline and forward-projected levels that extend the current cycle's grid into future bars.

Coto COP/TAH — Multi-Timeframe Bias Analyzer
tradingview.com/script/Bp4ls8mK · Pine Script v5
A dashboard table that reads 15m through yearly timeframes and derives directional bias from open-to-open comparisons. For each timeframe it classifies the candle pattern (normal/breakout bull or bear, sideways, anomaly), measures current range vs. average range, tracks how far the period has progressed through its cycle, and flags whether price has already made a new high/low confirming the bias.

Coto's Higher Timeframe (HTF) Candles Overlay — 2026 Edition
tradingview.com/script/xkGlOH8P · Pine Script v5
Renders up to 5 higher-timeframe candles directly on the current chart as box-drawn bodies and wicks, covering cycles from 2 years up to 250 years. Multi-year blocks can be anchored to a custom start year (useful for aligning to Bitcoin halvings or debt-cycle theory), and the currently-forming HTF candle is projected forward across the estimated remaining bars until its cycle closes.

04
Coto's COP/TAH — Auto Pivot + Auto Tick Size Detection
tradingview.com/script/N0yQ3cvY · Pine Script v5
Calculates the COP/TAH pivot formula (a 5-point average of the previous period's open/high/low plus current open, offset by one tick) to plot a pivot point with up to 5 support and 5 resistance levels, across 3 independent timeframe sets simultaneously. Tick size — critical to the formula's offset — is auto-detected from the symbol type (forex majors vs. JPY pairs, crypto, gold, indices, stocks) or can be set manually.
COP/TAH Auto Pivot Auto Tick Size Smart Detection

Bonds vs FFR
tradingview.com/script/sGFB69aL · Pine Script v5

The spread between the two drives a regime classifier — OVERTIGHT, ZIRP/QE, TIGHT, or NORMAL — with a live dashboard, colored histogram, and crossover alerts for recession-risk and tightening/easing signals.
